## Working and committed (up to d8c980c9b, everything builds)

- **The goal.** Chrome renders WebGL on the RTX 3060 and shows on the 3060's own monitor (an LG TV on HDMI). The customer confirmed the renderer line names Vulkan and the RTX 3060.
  - Log: `~/.local/share/ferrix/nvidia/n6-first-chrome-on-3060-monitor.log`, sha256 09d79b54…
  - Command: `cargo xtask run-compositor --nvidia --arch x86_64 --timeout 1800`.
  - The TV must be on and set to the 3060's input. A TV in standby drops HPD; nvrm then rescans every 2 s.
- **NVKMS in nvrm's core.**
  - `rm-kms.o` is built in the Makefile.
  - Ported files: `os/kept/nvidia-modeset.c` and `os/kept/nv-modeset-interface.c`.
  - `os/glue/kms.c` does KAPI modeset, the display watcher, and serves the card.
- **displayctl v8.** HELLO carries a `copies` flag. The kernel grants READ|MAP only when the chardev core publishes for the same device, and otherwise refuses with `Refusal::Copies`. nvrm copies each frame into VRAM: about 1.5 ms per frame with `rep movsb`.
- **Device units in init.** `devfs::announce` raises inotify events, and init has `.device` units. `hyprix.service` has `Requires=`/`After=dev-dri-card0.device`; the customer asked for ordering to be init's job.
- **PIN_CONTIGUOUS.** It replaces nvos's retry loop. That loop failing was what broke `vkCreateDevice`.
- **The ISR thread** keeps one CPU number (RM's `thread_state.c:963` assert).

## Speed

- WebGL runs at about 44 fps, and about 48 with the 8 vCPUs now set.
- Copy and flip are no longer the limit. The limit is Chrome's software path: GPU readback plus CPU compositing.
- The fix is N3b.

**Done, in nvrm:**
- `os/glue/drm.c`: the nvidia-drm subset over KAPI.
  - DRM ioctls: VERSION, GET_CAP, GEM_CLOSE, PRIME_HANDLE_TO_FD, PRIME_FD_TO_HANDLE.
  - NVIDIA ioctls: GET_DEV_INFO, GEM_ALLOC_NVKMS_MEMORY (pitch-linear goes to sysmem), GEM_IMPORT_NVKMS_MEMORY, GEM_EXPORT_NVKMS_MEMORY, GEM_MAP_OFFSET, GEM_IDENTIFY_OBJECT, DMABUF_SUPPORTED, GET_DRM_FILE_UNIQUE_ID.
- `chardev.c` routes `NVRM_RENDER_MINOR` (0x1000) and `NVOS_REQUEST_DMABUF_RELEASE` (op 5).
- `nv.c` adds `nvrm_alloc_at` (the live-allocation registry) and `nv_get_phys_pages`, which writes page addresses.
- `kms.c` exports `nvrm_kms_kapi` and `nvrm_kms_device`.
- nvos `chardev.rs` adds `nvos_chardev_dmabuf_install` / `nvos_chardev_dmabuf_resolve`.
- `main.c` publishes minors {255, 0, 0x1000}.

**Done, in xtask:** Chrome under `--nvidia` drops `--disable-gpu-compositing`. libgbm finds `gbm/nvidia-drm_gbm.so` through the `/usr/lib/x86_64-linux-gnu` link.
