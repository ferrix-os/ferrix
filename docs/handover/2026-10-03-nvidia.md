## What the next session does first

1. **Decide how a 13 MB nvrm is loaded.** RM's text alone is 12.2 MB. The kernel
   reads every driver into one heap `Vec` (`fs/mod.rs` `read_file`), and the
   heap's largest block is 4 MiB (`MAX_ORDER` 10); `process_create` also caps an
   image at 16 MiB. That is why N1b's unstripped 10 MB nvrm failed stage 10 with
   FX-1006. Options: (a) read drivers and images page by page and raise the cap,
   a kernel change in the load ring that needs the consultant; (b) keep nvrm
   small and load RM from the NVIDIA volume at run time; (c) start nvrm through
   the personality's exec path, which maps a program at any size (N1b chose
   `process_create` to keep the job, death watch and image-as-memory rule).
   This is a design decision to put to the customer and the consultant before
   N1c's link.
2. **Finish N1c** on `nvidia-n1c`, in the order its WIP commit lists: the
   remaining loud stubs (`glue/stubs.c`), the Makefile, compiling the kept C,
   `nvrm-link-test` (RM's no-GPU init up to `NV01_DEVICE_0` refused), the host
   and Ferrix runs, a `test-nvrm-link` gate, check, docs.
3. **N1d, GSP boot on the 3060**, is the first step on the real card:
   * the customer runs, once, as root, so the `ferrix-3060` domain uses the
     patched QEMU:
     `sudo sh -c 'rm -rf /usr/local/lib/ferrix/qemu && install -d -m 755 /usr/local/lib/ferrix/qemu && cp -r --no-preserve=ownership /home/sebastian/.local/share/ferrix/qemu/. /usr/local/lib/ferrix/qemu/'`;
   * GameLab, win11, manjaro, manjaro-kde-test and manjaro-xfce-test shut off
     (`virsh -c qemu:///system list --all` each time); never the RTX 3090;
     every write to the card listed and approved first;
   * at the first boot, record the `device_isolation` value nvrm prints
     (expected 0x3) and the date in NVIDIA.md §10 and under F-57's closure
     (the consultant's condition, ledger 279/284).
