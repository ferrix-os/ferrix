Stopped on the customer's word ("hard stop, then push"). Every branch named
here is pushed and none is landed.

## Measured on the card today

- **Teeworlds 0.7.5, vsync off, as the customer read it on the TV: 140 to
  150 frames a second, 160 at most; in full screen around 75 to 80.** The
  swap counter's one run (30 one-second samples, an LD_PRELOAD on
  glXSwapBuffers) gave median 147, lowest 72, highest 163
  (`~/.local/share/ferrix/linux-baseline/results/ferrix/tw-vsync0.swap`);
  whether that run's window was full screen was not checked. Branch
  `nv-cardvm`; the game draws with Mesa's software GL through yserver, and
  hyprix composites on the 3060 through virgl_test_server.
- The compositor shows 43 to 52 of those frames a second while the game runs;
  Chrome's WebGL page alone reads 56 to 60.
- AV1 1080p24 on Vulkan Video, FFmpeg to bgr0: 54 frames a second, no error
  (rav1d: 9 to 11). Branch `nv-next`, headless.
- `vkCreateDevice` with all 29 queues, six times in a row, after `nv-mapslots`.

The comparison against Linux on the same card was asked for and is **not
done**: the Debian guest under `~/.local/share/ferrix/linux-baseline/` is
unfinished (its agent was stopped mid-build) and no Linux number exists.
