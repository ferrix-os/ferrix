## What it is

- `credentials::may_access` is Linux's `__ptrace_may_access`, with
  `Process::attributes_pending` covering the newborn-child window.
- `/proc/<pid>/root`, `cwd`, `exe`, `fd`, `fdinfo`, `maps` and every `ns/*`
  link ask it, both read and followed.
- `ns/net` is new since the rebase onto netns: netns had landed
  `net::netns_file::may_open` with the pre-NP rule, which compares ids only
  and ignores dumpability. It now asks `may_access`.
- `get_robust_list` of another process's thread is `EPERM`.
