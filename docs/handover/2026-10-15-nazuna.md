## Lessons, also in the product-owner skill

The night spread ten agents over about twenty workstreams while the goal had
two, and spent about 30% of the weekly usage mostly on agents waiting for
queued gates. Staff the critical path first, leave slots empty when the pool
is saturated, test the real workload in a small guest before landing its
fix, and have agents report only when red or ready.
